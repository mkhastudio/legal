The ritual

1 · Paper

Take or choose a photo. It rests faintly on brush-coated, yellow-green paper.

2 · Expose

Hold your iPhone toward real light: a bright sky or a lamp, never the sun. The brighter the light, the faster the print exposes.

3 · Wash

Shake to wash, or tap Wash. The yellow rinses away and Prussian blue appears.

4 · Dry

The print keeps deepening for about a day, in the app and on a Home Screen widget, then goes into your print box.

Free, and Darkroom

Sunblue is free to use, with a small corner stamp and a print box for your latest prints. Sunblue Darkroom is a one-time purchase that removes the stamp, keeps every print and adds washi, double coat and tea-toned papers and toners.

Private

No account, no server, no analytics. Your photos never leave your iPhone.
