How it works

1 · Snap

Photograph a can badge, an acrylic stand or a photocard. Saidan lifts it out of the photo on your iPhone. You can also frame a card with rounded corners, trace around a piece, and tidy the edges.

2 · Display

Stands line up on lit glass shelves, badges pin into a clear-window ita-bag, photocards slide into nine-pocket binder pages. Holo and acrylic shine as you tilt your iPhone.

3 · Count

Add a price and see what this month cost, in your own currency. Add a live, a release or a birthday and Saidan counts down the days, on the Home Screen widget too.

Free, and Saidan Plus

One oshi shelf with 30 pieces is free. Saidan Plus is a one-time purchase for unlimited oshi and merch and three extra shelf themes. No subscription.

Made up, on purpose

The sample shelf shows MOFUMOON, a group invented for the app and drawn in code. Saidan contains no official images of any artist or brand: you photograph your own goods.

Private

No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Your photos and lists stay on your iPhone.
