Common questions

I bought Plus but it is locked. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription? iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. See our Privacy Policy.

The widget looks out of date. Long-press the widget, remove it and add it again, or open the app once.
