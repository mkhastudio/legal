Reversi that breaks its own rules: a 15-minute roguelike.

Support · Privacy

A run

1 · Trap to turn

Classic reversi on a small board. Each disc you turn in a move is worth more than the last, and tempo multiplies the move.

2 · Take a tile

Clear a board, then take one of three tiles that bend the rules for the rest of the run: Lantern, Echo, Mirror, Prism, Anchor, Tide, Ratchet, Compound.

3 · Beat the boss

The fifth board of each act has a boss: Eclipse removes four squares, Quarter Turn rotates the board.

Free, and the Full Game

Act 1 and the daily run are free. The Full Game is a one-time purchase that opens Act 2 (8×8 boards and the Quarter Turn boss) and the Edge and Centre schools.

Private

No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Your runs stay on your iPhone.
