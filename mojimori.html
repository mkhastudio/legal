How it plays

Drop

Tap to drop an ink stone into a paper jar. Each stone carries a kanji part.

Fuse

Parts that belong together fuse when they touch: 木＋木＝林, 林＋木＝森, 日＋月＝明. Chain fusions for a bigger score before the jar overflows.

Collect

Every kanji you make goes into the Book, with its reading and meaning. 41 kanji in three jars, plus a Daily Jar that is the same for everyone.

Free, and Mojimori Full

The Forest Jar, the Daily Jar and the Book are free. Mojimori Full is a one-time purchase that opens the Sun & Moon Jar and the People Jar.

Private

No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Your Book and scores never leave your iPhone.
