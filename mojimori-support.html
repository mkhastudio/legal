Common questions

How do parts fuse? By real composition, not by matching: 木＋木＝林, 林＋木＝森, 亻＋木＝休. If two touching parts make a kanji in this jar, they fuse. You can replay the introduction from Settings.

A kanji in the Book shows “Not found yet”. It is made in another jar, or from a part that falls in another jar. The hint shows its first part and the jar it belongs to.

I bought Mojimori Full but the jars are locked. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. If purchases are turned off on your iPhone, check Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Is Mojimori Full a subscription? No. It is a one-time purchase that opens the Sun & Moon Jar and the People Jar. The Forest Jar, the Daily Jar and the Book stay free. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I change the language? In the app: Settings › Language opens Mojimori’s page in iPhone Settings, where you can pick the app’s language. The list appears when your iPhone has more than one preferred language (Settings › General › Language & Region). Mojimori is available in English, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Thai, Indonesian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French and German.

Can I play with VoiceOver? Yes. Swipe up or down on the jar to move the aim, double-tap to drop; each drop and fusion is announced.

Where are my Book and scores? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. See our Privacy Policy.
