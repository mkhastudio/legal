Common questions

The print exposes very slowly. Hold the screen toward brighter light, such as a window on a bright day or a lamp. Never point it at the sun. In dim rooms you can tap Wash now to finish early.

I can’t or don’t want to shake. Tap Wash instead, or turn shaking off in Settings. With VoiceOver or Switch Control one activation washes the whole print.

Sunblue can’t use the camera. Allow it in iPhone Settings › Sunblue › Camera. You can still choose a photo from your library or use the built-in samples.

Saving to Photos fails. Allow Sunblue to add photos in iPhone Settings › Sunblue › Photos.

I bought Darkroom but it is locked. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is Darkroom a subscription? No. It is a one-time purchase.

The widget looks out of date. Open the app once, or remove the widget and add it again.

Where are my prints? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. See our Privacy Policy.
