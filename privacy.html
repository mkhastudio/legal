Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Applies to all Wickmoss apps listed below.

The short version

Our apps do not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. What you create stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we cannot see your notes, photos, closet, progress or focus history.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you own an unlock, so it can turn on Plus features. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

Camera and Photos

Where an app uses the camera, images are processed on your device and are not uploaded. Apps only add to your Photos library when you choose to save something, and they cannot read your library.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Our apps are suitable for all ages and collect no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.

App details

Cozy Focus

  • Focus sessions, your village and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • If you choose to save a time-lapse, it is written to your Photos library only when you tap Save.

Tealeaf

  • Your decks, progress and streak are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Room Hunt uses the camera to recognise objects. Images are analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • Word posters are saved to Photos only when you tap Save Image.

Coathook

  • Clothing photos and outfits are stored only on your iPhone.
  • The camera is used only when you photograph a garment. Background removal runs on your iPhone.
  • Images and videos are saved to Photos only when you tap Save.

Magnet

  • Your notes, magnets and reminders are stored only on your iPhone (and its widgets).
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

Changes and contact
