Get help

Email mkha.studio@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within two days.

Common questions

The cut-out missed part of my piece. Use Restore to paint it back, or Erase to remove the table. Clear acrylic and glossy sleeves are the hardest: photograph one piece at a time on a plain, contrasting surface. For photocards, Card frames the card with rounded corners.

How do I remove the sample shelf? Settings › Remove sample merch. Your own merch stays. Tap the shelf title › Edit oshi to rename the oshi.

My spending shows the wrong currency. Settings › Currency. Totals count merch priced in the chosen currency.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search for Saidan, and choose small or medium.

I bought Saidan Plus but it is locked. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is Saidan Plus a subscription? No. It is a one-time purchase. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I change the language? Settings › Language opens the app’s page in iOS Settings.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. See our Privacy Policy.
